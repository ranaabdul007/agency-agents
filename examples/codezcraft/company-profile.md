Custom Accounting & Manufacturing ERP connecting purchasing, sales, inventory, production, and subcontracting with double-entry accounting.
- Finance and accounting: chart of accounts, vouchers, receivables, payables, cash and bank, tax, fixed assets, accounting periods.
- Purchasing and sales: goods receipts, supplier bills, customer deliveries, sales invoices.
- Inventory and production: raw materials, finished goods, production work orders, WIP tracking.
- Job work and subcontracting.
- Reporting and controls: financial and operational reports, role-based access, transaction cancellation rules.

Use as a portfolio example for custom ERP, manufacturing, accounting, connected workflows. Keep claims about results, deployment status and project size grounded in what CodezCraft provides.

3. **Business websites and export catalogues**: mobile-friendly sites where overseas buyers browse collections and submit bulk quotation requests.
4. **Custom ERP and web applications**: internal apps, client portals, dashboards, reporting tools.
5. **API integrations and business automation**.

Tech: C#, ASP.NET Core MVC, REST APIs, SQL Server, React, PHP, Oracle, WPF.
